Our Heritage & Craft

The Spirit of Tyaari: The Art of Becoming Extraordinary

In Indian traditions, "Tyaari" represents the sacred, joyous hour of preparation before stepping onto life's grandest stages. It is the click of a bracelet, the weight of an heirloom earring against your cheek, and the undeniable surge of confidence as you catch your reflection.

Born from the regal courtyards of Rajasthan and reimagined for contemporary celebrations, Tyaari Jewellery bridges generational Polki and Kundan artistry with featherlight modern wearability. Each piece undergoes 45 days of meticulous stone cutting, foil backing, and multi-layer anti-tarnish gilding.
